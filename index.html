Privacy Policy

Your videos are personal. This page explains how Video Clipper handles media, app permissions, advertising, and subscriptions.

Effective date: October 6, 2026  ·  Android package: com.rbapps.videoclipper

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy describes the data practices of Video Clipper, an Android video editing application published by RB APPS ("we," "us," or "our"). It applies to use of the Video Clipper app, including its free, advertising-supported, rewarded-access, and paid Pro features.

2. Videos, audio, and edited content

Video Clipper requests access to media you select so that it can import, preview, edit, and export videos and audio. Editing and exporting are designed to take place on your device. Exported videos may be saved to your device's shared media storage so you can find, play, and share them.

We do not operate a video-upload or cloud-storage backend for your editing projects in the current app setup. Your selected media is not intentionally uploaded by us to a Video Clipper server. If you choose to share an exported file using another app or service, that destination handles the file according to its own privacy practices.

3. Information processed by the app

  • Media and files: videos, audio, and related file information you choose to open or export.
  • App preferences: editing settings, locally stored Pro reward progress, and temporary Pro expiration information.
  • Purchase information: subscription product, purchase status, and entitlement information made available through Google Play Billing. We do not receive your full payment-card number.
  • Advertising and technical data: when ads are used, Google's advertising services may process IP address, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, and diagnostic information.

Advertising-related information may be used for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and service reliability. IP addresses can also be used to estimate general location.

4. Android permissions

Depending on Android version and app functionality, Video Clipper may use media or storage access to open selected video/audio files and save exports; internet and network-state access for ads and purchase services; notification permission for export or processing notifications; and foreground-service or wake-lock capabilities to support ongoing media processing. Permissions are requested or used only when relevant to supported features.

You can review and change applicable permissions in Android Settings. Restricting certain permissions may limit related features.

5. Google AdMob and rewarded ads

The free version of Video Clipper uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google's Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, and device/account identifiers, subject to applicable settings, consent requirements, and Google's policies.

Video Clipper may offer an optional reward: completing five eligible rewarded advertisements can unlock temporary Pro access for 24 hours. In the current version, reward progress and temporary-access timing are stored locally on the device; a separate Video Clipper reward-verification backend is not in use. Ad delivery and measurement are handled through Google's advertising services.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information for advertising.

6. Google Play subscriptions

Video Clipper may offer an optional recurring Pro subscription through Google Play Billing. Google Play manages payment processing, billing, subscription renewals, and cancellations. The app checks purchase or entitlement status to enable Pro features. Pricing, billing terms, and available options are displayed in the Google Play purchase flow.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions through Google Play subscriptions. Deleting the app does not automatically cancel an active subscription.

7. Why information is used

  • To import, edit, render, and export media you choose.
  • To remember app settings and manage temporary rewarded Pro access.
  • To verify and provide purchased Pro features.
  • To display and measure ads, subject to applicable privacy settings and consent.
  • To maintain security, prevent abuse, and troubleshoot service issues.

8. Third-party services and sharing

Video Clipper integrates services provided by Google, including Google AdMob and Google Play Billing. These providers may independently process information necessary to deliver their services. We do not sell your imported videos or exported media. Other sharing of video files happens when you choose to export or share them using your device.

9. Storage, retention, and deletion

Imported and edited media, exported videos, and locally saved app settings remain on your device according to Android storage behavior and your actions. You can delete exported files using your device's file manager or gallery. You can clear locally stored app preferences through Android app settings; uninstalling the app generally removes its private app data, although files exported to shared storage may remain.

Google may retain advertising and purchase records under its own policies. To request information about data we may hold or ask a privacy question, use the contact method below.

10. Security

We aim to limit access to selected media and rely on Android platform controls for local files and permissions. Google services handle their own network communications and security practices. No method of data storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Children's privacy

Video Clipper is not specifically designed for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us using the method below so we can review the concern.

12. Your choices and rights

You can manage app permissions in Android Settings, delete local media and exported files, manage Google advertising preferences using available device or Google controls, and manage Pro subscriptions through Google Play. You may contact us with privacy questions or requests relating to data we control.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if features, data practices, or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.
